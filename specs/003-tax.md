# SPEC-003 Tax

Tax applies once per invoice, on the amount after discounts. The rate is in basis points.

- **003.1** Tax is the taxable amount times the rate, rounded half up to a whole cent. Example: 10001 cents at 2000 basis points is 2000.2 cents, so 2000 cents.
- **003.2** A rate of 0 gives a tax of 0.
- **003.3** A rate above 10000 basis points, below 0, or not a whole number is rejected with the error code `INVALID_TAX_RATE`.
